# Hi, I'm Ben 👋

## About Me

I'm a marine science student at James Cook University, currently working through
MB5370 and building a technical toolkit to support a career in animal care and
conservation. My interests sit at the intersection of ecology, animal behaviour,
and hands-on conservation work — and increasingly, the data and coding skills
that let marine scientists turn observations into evidence.

I'm hoping to begin an internship with SEA LIFE Kelly Tarlton's in Auckland this
September, and my goal from there is to move into a permanent role with them —
bringing together practical animal care experience with the technical skills
I've been developing through this coursework.

This profile brings together the repositories I've built across MB5370, tracking
everything from foundational R programming to genomic analysis of coral disease.
